**Project Name:** Automated Dental Appointment Reminder & Intake System  
**Date:** October 2026  
**Interviewer:** Business Analyst  
**Interviewees:** 
- Sarah Jenkins (Front-Desk Lead)
- Dr. Aris (Lead Dentist)
- Marcus Vance (Billing & Clinic Operations Manager)

---

### Executive Context
The dental network is currently facing a **25% appointment no-show rate**, causing open chair time, inefficient schedule utilization, and significant daily revenue loss across clinical locations. This discovery session was conducted to uncover front-line operational pain points contributing to missed appointments and check-in delays.

---

### Key Findings by Stakeholder

#### 1. Front Desk Operations (Sarah Jenkins)
* **Current Workflow:** Staff manually dial patients 24 to 48 hours prior to scheduled visits to confirm appointments. Paper medical history and intake forms are handed to patients on clipboards upon arrival.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Front-desk staff spend 3–4 hours daily making manual phone calls, most of which go directly to voicemail.
  * Paper forms take 10–15 minutes per patient to complete and manually re-enter into the practice management system.
  * Patients who arrive late to complete paper forms cause downstream delays for the entire clinical team.

#### 2. Clinical Care Team (Dr. Aris)
* **Current Workflow:** Dentists and hygienists rely on fixed daily schedules prepared the morning of service.
* **Pain Points:**
  * Last-minute no-shows leave clinical chairs empty with zero advance warning to pull from waitlists.
  * Patients frequently arrive without having followed pre-appointment care instructions (e.g., pre-medication or fasting for sedation), requiring appointments to be rescheduled.

#### 3. Billing & Operations (Marcus Vance)
* **Current Workflow:** Clinic performance is tracked by chair utilization and daily completed appointment counts.
* **Pain Points:**
  * The 25% no-show rate accounts for thousands of dollars in unrecovered operating overhead every week.
  * Lack of a central digital tracking dashboard makes it impossible to identify which patients are high-risk no-shows before the appointment day arrives.

---

### Summary of High-Level Business Needs
1. Implement an automated SMS and email confirmation system operating 48 hours prior to appointments.
2. Provide a mobile-friendly digital intake link allowing patients to submit medical history forms before arriving at the office.
3. Build a real-time front-desk dashboard to display confirmation and intake completion statuses across all scheduled appointments.
